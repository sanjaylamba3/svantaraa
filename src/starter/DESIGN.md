## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elevated editorial hospitality aesthetic inspired by Hamptons-style coastal sanctuaries and bespoke wellness resorts. It targets discerning pet parents seeking consummate care, peace of mind, and tasteful, quiet luxury for their companions. 

The visual identity merges **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile Editorial Craft**:
- **Generous Canvas Space:** Expansive breathing room reminiscent of high-end travel journals, evoking relaxation and open-air luxury.
- **Refined Balance:** High-contrast, editorial serif headlines anchored by clean, legible modern sans-serif typography for service details and bookings.
- **Understated Distinction:** Warm champagne accents and deep forest greens replacing loud, saturated playful pet clichés with timeless, serene sophistication.
- **Restraint and Craft:** Hairline dividers, subtle warm-tinted borders, and uncluttered cards that let photography and pastoral landscapes shine.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an organic, warm-light canvas paired with deep charcoal contrast and pastoral nature accents:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FAF8F5`): A luminous, warm linen off-white that eliminates clinical digital glare.
  - Surface Elevated / Card (`#FFFFFF`): Crisp pure white utilized for service cards, overlays, and floating headers.
  - Surface Subdued (`#F4F1EA`): Soft stone warm-sand tone used for section backgrounds, feature badges, and list borders.

- **Primary Charcoal (`#1A1918`):**
  - Deep espresso charcoal replacing pure `#000000` to deliver soft yet commanding editorial typography, primary action buttons, and refined headers.

- **Secondary Forest / Sage (`#2D4B3E`):**
  - Rich botanical deep green reflecting manicured lawns, natural agility parks, and therapeutic retreat environments. Used for active badges, focused states, and accent cards.

- **Tertiary Champagne Gold (`#C29B38` / `#D4A373`):**
  - Warm metallic champagne gold reserved for prestige accents, star ratings, premium service tiers, subtle hover glows, and luxury crests.

- **Borders & Dividers:**
  - Hairline Border (`#E6E1D8`): Muted, organic bone-grey for razor-thin outlines that avoid visual weight.

## Typography

The type scale combines architectural, editorial dignity with effortless functional clarity:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for primary storytelling, heroic brand banners, suite titles, and section leaders. Set in natural sentence case with slight negative tracking on large displays for an authentic publication mood.
- **Overlines & Section Trackers (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Small caps with wide tracking (`0.15em`) set in medium-weight sans-serif create high-end boutique hospitality structure before major headings.
- **Body & Microcopy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, open letterforms engineered for warm legibility across amenity lists, terms, itinerary tables, and booking forms.
- **Pricing & Numerical Accents:** Crisp sans-serif bold numerals paired with subtle regular-weight cadence indicators (e.g., `₹2,250 / night`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured **12-column fluid grid** flanked by generous margins, instilling calm and unhurried visual cadence:

- **Canvas Limits & Alignment:**
  - Maximum content width is capped at `1280px` for editorial balance.
  - Section paddings use expansive vertical rhythms (`space-2xl` to `5rem`) to mimic physical white linen print magazines.

- **Breakpoints & Adaptation:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `4rem` outer canvas margins. 3-column accommodation and service card rows.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `2rem` margins. 2-column card structures with fluid vertical flows.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` outer margins. Cards collapse into elegant vertical stacks or edge-peek horizontal snap carousels with visible peek margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eschews heavy drop shadows in favor of **ambient diffused shadows** and **low-contrast tactile outlines**:

- **Card Elevation:** Cards sit directly on the off-white canvas (`#FAF8F5`) surrounded by a warm hairline border (`1px solid #E6E1D8`) and an ultra-soft, warm-tinted ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Hover & Active States:** On hover, cards transition seamlessly with a subtle vertical translate (`-3px`) and expanded ambient warmth: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 25, 24, 0.08)`.
- **Floating Navigation Bar:** The header bar uses a frosted glass pill aesthetic (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with a translucent white background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), framed by a `1px` translucent champagne/bone border.
- **Separators:** All visual dividers are single-pixel continuous or dotted hairline rules (`#E6E1D8`), avoiding solid drop shadows or abrupt divides.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape philosophy, communicating friendliness and gentle organic comfort while maintaining architectural structure:

- **Cards & Enclosures:** Finished with `1rem` (`16px`) corners, balancing modern softness with editorial precision.
- **Imagery & Feature Photography:** Hero banners and card media use `0.75rem` (`12px`) to `1.25rem` (`20px`) rounded profiles to soften natural photography without turning cartoonish.
- **Buttons & Badges:** Primary interactive elements leverage generous organic curvature or full pill geometries (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for touch targets, floating navigation bars, and category status chips.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary Button:* Espresso Charcoal (`#1A1918`) fill, warm white text (`#FAF8F5`), pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Gentle hover lift with subtle tonal shift to `#2D4B3E` (Forest Green).
  - *Secondary / Outline Button:* Pure transparent background, `1px solid #1A1918` border, charcoal text. On hover, fills with light warm sand tint (`#F4F1EA`).
  - *Tertiary / Text Link:* Serif or tracked sans-serif text accompanied by a fine directional arrow (`Discover →`), animated with an underline growth or horizontal micro-nudge.

- **Service & Retreat Cards:**
  - Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed with `1px solid #E6E1D8` and `1rem` radius.
  - High-aspect-ratio photography (`16:10` or `4:3`) with soft rounded corners.
  - Accompanied by a Playfair Display heading, 2-line description, bulleted amenity list featuring bespoke micro paw or botanical icons in muted sage (`#2D4B3E`), and a bottom anchored footer containing the daily rate and a pill "Book Now" CTA.

- **Chips & Status Badges:**
  - Rendered in pill form with subtle sand backgrounds (`#F4F1EA`), refined typography (`label-sm`), and delicate warm green borders for active retreat perks (e.g., *“Private Garden”*, *“Supervised Play”*, *“Under Renovation”*).

- **Form Fields & Booking Inputs:**
  - Clean surface inputs set on `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E6E1D8` border, `0.75rem` radius, and `1rem` internal padding.
  - Focus state highlights with a crisp `#2D4B3E` (Forest Green) hairline border and a subtle `#C29B38` (Champagne Gold) focus ring (`2px`).
  - Labels positioned above in `title-editorial` small caps.

- **Checkboxes & Selection Controls:**
  - Custom square checks with soft `4px` rounding; selected state fills with deep green (`#2D4B3E`) containing an ivory checkmark.
  - Radio buttons feature a clean concentric champagne or forest green circle design.

- **Bespoke Resort Amenities Ribbon (Marquee):**
  - A full-bleed ivory band featuring understated hairline top and bottom dividers. Repeating retreat amenities separated by refined paw crests or bronze diamond icons set in muted charcoal.
